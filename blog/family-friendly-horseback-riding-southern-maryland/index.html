Family-Friendly Horseback Riding in Southern Maryland

A Day Out Everyone Remembers

Local Trails & Rides October 12, 2025 5 min read By Savanna

Family-friendly horseback riding in Southern Maryland is one of the best screen-free outings a family can share, and it is far more accessible than many parents realize. You do not need to own a horse, own gear, or have any experience at all.

This guide explains why horses make such a wonderful family activity, what to expect, and how to choose the right farm in Anne Arundel County.

Key takeaways

  • Horseback riding is a rare activity that spans the generations.
  • Guided trail rides are the easiest family starting point.
  • No horse, gear or experience is needed to begin.
  • Family-friendly farms keep numbers small and horses calm.
  • It is an active, outdoor, genuinely memorable day out.

Why horses make a great family activity

Riding is one of the few activities that genuinely works across the generations. A grandparent, a parent and a child can all ride the same gentle trail, side by side, and each take something different and meaningful from the hour.

It is calming and active at the same time. It gets everyone outdoors, off their phones, and sharing a real experience rather than a screen — and the photos at the end are the kind families keep for years.

Guided trail rides versus lessons

Families generally start in one of two ways. For a relaxed first outing, a guided trail ride is ideal — no instruction, no pressure, just a gentle ride through the woods together with a guide leading the way.

If a child falls for horses — and many do — riding lessons are the natural next step. Private and semi-private lessons build real skill, balance and confidence, week by week, at the child's own pace.

What makes a farm genuinely family-friendly

Look for calm, well-matched horses chosen for temperament; small group sizes and real attention; a patient, experienced instructor or guide; a quiet, low-pressure setting; and clear safety guidance with properly fitted helmets. Trust your instincts when you visit.

What a family ride feels like

At Solitude Stables, a family arrives down a quiet gravel lane to quiet wooded acres hidden a mile from the road. The guide introduces each horse, covers a few simple safety basics, and helps everyone mount up.

Then the group sets off together through the forest at a gentle, walking pace, with the guide alongside the whole way. Nobody is rushed, nobody is left behind, and the loudest sound is birdsong.

What is a good first horse activity for families?

A guided trail ride is an excellent first horse activity for families. It requires no experience or equipment, suits a wide range of ages, and lets everyone share a calm, memorable hour outdoors together. Lessons are a natural next step for anyone who wants to learn to ride.

Making it a tradition

Many South County families return to riding again and again — a seasonal trail ride, a birthday outing, a standing weekend lesson. Horses have a quiet way of becoming part of a family's story.

Because there are no contracts or membership fees, families can ride as often or as occasionally as life allows, and still feel welcomed back every time.

Years from now, your children will not remember a screen. They will remember the horse.

Plan your family day

Whether it is a one-time adventure or the start of a lifelong love, family-friendly horseback riding in Southern Maryland gives every generation something to share — and a guided ride through our Harwood woods is the perfect place to begin.
