the easy part

What happens next

No apps, no phone tag, no pressure — just a friendly back-and-forth by email until you’re booked.

1

We read your note

Your request comes right to us. We’ll match the rider with the right horse and the right lesson or trail.

2

We reply by email

Usually within a day, with a suggested day & time, what to wear and bring, and simple directions to the farm.

3

You come ride

Arrive a few minutes early, meet your horse, and settle in. We’ll take it at your pace from the very first step.
