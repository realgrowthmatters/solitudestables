Best Horse Riding Trails Near Annapolis, MD

Where to Ride, From Our Gate to the Mountains

If you have ever wondered where you can actually ride a horse around Annapolis and Anne Arundel County, this is our honest, local guide to the best horse riding trails near us — from the sandy paths of Jug Bay, minutes from our farm, to the rugged miles of Patapsco. We ride this country ourselves, and we keep this list grounded in real, current sources.

The shortlist

Our favorite places to ride near Annapolis

Ranked roughly by how close they are to South County — because the best ride is often the one you can reach before the morning dew burns off.

1. Jug Bay Natural Area, Patuxent River Park

Lothian, MD · ~10 min from us · Beginner–Intermediate · Pull-through trailer lot

The closest real trail riding to our gate, and a local favorite. More than eight miles of quiet, sandy woodland trails wind above the Patuxent, and the lot is sized for trailers — as riders like to say, you do not have to back up. The narrow, winding blue trail is the one regulars rave about.

Good to know: Sand & dirt; gentle. Port-a-john at the boat ramp. Closest option to Harwood.

BeginnersIntermediateNature loversLocals

Sources: Trail Riders of Today — Jug Bay · Find Your Chesapeake — Patuxent River Park / Jug Bay

2. Merkle Natural Resources Management Area

Upper Marlboro, MD · ~25 min · Beginner · Roadside / lot access

Just upriver from Jug Bay, Merkle is open and gentle — a two-mile stretch of the Chesapeake Bay Critical Area route is open to horses, linking into the wider Patuxent River Park lands. Big-sky meadows, geese on the water, and an easy pace make it a lovely, low-key outing.

Good to know: Flat, easy going; part of a 4-mile hike/bike/ride/drive route from Patuxent River Park.

BeginnersSeniorsEasy & scenic

Sources: Maryland DNR — Merkle driving tour

3. Patapsco Valley State Park

Central MD · ~50 min · Intermediate–Advanced · Designated horse lots

The big one. Patapsco sprawls along its namesake river with 170-plus miles of trail and roughly eleven routes open to horses across its areas. Rocky climbs, river crossings and real elevation reward a fit horse and a confident rider — this is where you go to feel like you have been somewhere.

Good to know: Hilly, rocky, varied; check the wet-weather closures before you load up.

IntermediateAdvancedAdventure seekers

Sources: Maryland DNR — Patapsco Valley horseback riding

4. Seneca Creek S.P. & Schaeffer Farm Trails

Montgomery Co. · ~75 min · Intermediate · Schaeffer Farm horse lot

A perennial pick in reader polls of Maryland riders. Between Seneca Creek and the adjoining Schaeffer Farm system you will find 50-plus miles of well-marked, rolling trail through woods and farmland — enough to ride a different loop every visit.

Good to know: Rolling, well-marked; popular, so expect company on weekends.

IntermediateLong ridesGroups

Sources: The Equiery — Maryland public lands with equestrian trails

5. Gunpowder Falls State Park — Sweet Air

Baltimore Co. · ~70 min · Intermediate · Sweet Air horse parking

The Sweet Air area sets aside about eighteen miles of trail for riders, with long sight-lines over fields and river bottom. It is a sightseer's ride — steady, pretty, and a little wild at the edges.

Good to know: Mixed woods and field; gentle to moderate. Quietest midweek.

IntermediateSceneryHalf-day rides

Sources: The Equiery — Maryland public lands with equestrian trails

6. Patuxent River State Park (Hipsley Mill)

Howard/Montgomery · ~75 min · Advanced · Hipsley Mill Rd lot

Wild and undeveloped — 6,700 acres and 25-plus miles of natural-surface trail with stream crossings and few amenities. Bring everything you need and a horse that is sure of foot; the reward is genuine solitude.

Good to know: Rugged, natural; minimal facilities — carry water and a map.

AdvancedSolitudeAdventure

Sources: The Equiery — Maryland public lands with equestrian trails

7. Anne Arundel County Trails (Pasadena)

Pasadena, MD · ~45 min · Beginner–Intermediate · Gated trailer lots

Closer to home, the county opens unpaved trails to horses at the Lake Shore Athletic Complex and the Magothy Greenway at Looper's Field. Handy, well-kept and beginner-friendly — just mind the county rules: sunrise to sunset, walk only, designated trails, and no riding when the ground is wet.

Good to know: Easy, maintained; trailer parking in the gated upper lot at Lake Shore.

BeginnersLocalsShort rides

Sources: Anne Arundel County — horseback riding trails

8. Charles County Equestrian Trails

Southern MD · ~50 min · Beginner–Intermediate · Park trailer parking

A short hop south, Charles County maintains dedicated equestrian trails that fly under most riders' radar — which is rather the point. Quiet, pleasant and rarely crowded.

Good to know: Gentle, uncrowded; confirm current access before you go.

BeginnersQuiet ridesOff the beaten path

Sources: Charles County Rec & Parks — equestrian trails

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick, honest answers to the questions we hear most.

The closest public option is Jug Bay Natural Area in Patuxent River Park (Lothian), about ten minutes from Harwood, with 8-plus miles of sandy woodland trail and trailer-friendly parking. Farther afield, Patapsco Valley, Seneca Creek/Schaeffer Farm, Gunpowder Falls (Sweet Air) and Patuxent River State Park all offer miles of horse trail.

Jug Bay and Merkle, near the Patuxent, are gentle, scenic and easy to navigate, which makes them ideal for newer riders and quiet outings. Anne Arundel County's Pasadena trails are also beginner-friendly and well maintained.

Some cross natural streams, but you should never rely on it. Carry your own water, and do not depend on wild forage — pack what your horse needs, especially in summer.

Yes — these are public trails for riders bringing their own horse. If you do not own a horse, a guided, instructor-led trail ride for two at Solitude Stables is the easiest way to experience our wooded Southern Maryland trails.
