A warm welcome

A little farm with a big, gentle heart

Welcome to Solitude Stables, a small family-run farm offering beginner-friendly horseback riding lessons and guided trail rides in Harwood, Maryland, just 13 miles south of Annapolis. I am Savanna, and teaching people to ride — really ride, with confidence and kindness — has been my life's work for more than fifteen years.

Our farm is hidden a mile from the road, surrounded by open fields and forest. It is the kind of place where a nervous first-timer can take a deep breath, meet a patient horse, and discover they are braver than they thought.

Whether your child is brand new to horses or already horse-crazy, we will meet them exactly where they are — no pressure, no rushing, just steady encouragement.

Your ride should be the best part of your day or week — the hour you look forward to the most.”

— Savanna, Owner & Instructor

Why families choose us

The Solitude Stables difference

We are not a big commercial barn, and we never want to be. Here is what that means for you.

Truly personal attention

Lessons are private or semi-private, so your instructor is focused on one or two riders — never a crowded ring.

Confidence comes first

We move at the rider's pace. No pressure, no rushing — just steady, patient encouragement every step.

Scenic wooded trails

Miles of peaceful forest trails make our farm a true trail rider's paradise, right here in southern Maryland.

Gentle, well-matched horses

Each horse is chosen for a calm temperament, and we carefully match every rider with the right partner.

A genuinely experienced teacher

With 20+ years riding and 15+ years teaching, plus a Master's in Conservation, Savanna brings real depth to every lesson.

An eco-minded family farm

We run primarily on solar power and care for our land as thoughtfully as we care for our horses.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick, honest answers to the questions we hear most.

Yes. Most riders who come to Solitude Stables have never sat on a horse before, and that is exactly who we love to teach. Every lesson is private or semi-private, so your instructor stays right beside you, and we begin with calm groundwork and the basics of balance long before any trotting. Beginners of every age are warmly welcome.

We welcome children, teens and adults. As a gentle guide, most children are ready for a first lesson around age six or seven, once they can follow simple instructions and feel comfortable near a large animal. We are always happy to talk through your child's readiness before you book.

Our farm sits on peaceful wooded acres at 4800 Solomons Island Rd, Harwood, MD 20776, about 13 miles south of Annapolis and hidden a mile off the road. We welcome riders from Edgewater, Davidsonville, Crofton, Severna Park and across Anne Arundel County.

Horseback riding lessons start at $55 for a 45-minute semi-private lesson, and guided trail rides are $75 for a 60-minute ride. Every option is listed on our pricing page, and there are no membership fees or surprise charges.

Long pants and closed-toe shoes or boots with a small heel are perfect for a first lesson. A properly fitted riding helmet is required for every rider, and we are glad to help with sizing. Bringing a water bottle and a sense of adventure rounds out the list.

Yes. Our guided trail rides wind through quiet, wooded trails and are paced for the riders in your group, which makes them a lovely outing for families, beginners and visiting friends. Children and adults are both welcome on the trail.
