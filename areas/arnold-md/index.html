Horseback Riding Lessons Near Arnold, Maryland

Worth the Scenic Drive Down County

Solitude Stables offers warm, beginner-friendly horseback riding lessons and guided trail rides for families near Arnold, MD, from our wooded countryside farm in nearby Harwood.

Arnold sits north of Annapolis on the Broadneck peninsula, close to the Bay and the B&A Trail. It is a bit further from our farm — and Arnold families tell us the drive south is half the relaxation.

By the time you have wound through South County's farmland, the calm of the trail ride has already begun.

Why Arnold families ride with us

Calm, personal lessons

Private and semi-private lessons mean Arnold riders get real, unhurried attention every visit.
