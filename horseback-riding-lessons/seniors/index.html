Horseback Riding Lessons for Seniors in Harwood, MD

Gentle, Unhurried, and Good for You

Why it suits older riders

Riding that gives back

An unhurried, gentle pace

We build everything around comfort — calm horses, a mounting block, and as much time as you like. Nothing here is rushed.

Balance, mobility and mood

Riding gently challenges balance and core in a joyful way, and the bond with a horse lifts the spirits like little else.

Honest about what works

We will talk frankly about mobility and comfort, and shape lessons — or even unmounted horsemanship — to suit you.

+5.2 points

balance improvement in older riders

In a study of older participants, a horse-riding program improved Berg Balance Scale scores by 5.2 points, and a 2024 meta-analysis found equine-assisted activity enhanced gait and strength in older adults. Good for the body, wonderful for the soul.

Source: NIH / PMC meta-analysis (2024) & balance research
Made comfortable

Small touches that matter

A sturdy mounting block and a leg-up if you would like one — no undignified scrambling.

Gentle, steady horses and short, frequent rests whenever you want them.

Prefer to keep both feet on the ground at first? Grooming and groundwork are a lovely, valuable way to begin.
